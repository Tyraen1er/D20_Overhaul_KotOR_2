## A Clear Identity for Every Armor Category

In vanilla KotOR 2, wearing armor as a Jedi was almost always a trap: it blocked most Force powers while providing less Defense than simple robes with high Dexterity.

**D20 Overhaul** gives **every armor category** a distinct role through **native Damage Reduction (DR)**, updated Defense and Max DEX caps, tailored workbench slots, and **three dedicated armor feat trees**.

| Category | Base AC *(before → after)* | Max DEX *(before → after)* | Native DR *(Physical & Energy)* | Workbench Upgrade Slots | Allowed Force Powers |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Robes & Clothing** *(Unarmored)* | `0` *(unchanged)* | Unlimited *(except restrictive robes +5)* | **0** | None *(0 slots)* | **All powers** |
| **Light Armor** | `4–5` → **`6–7`** | `+4/+5` → **`+7 to +10`** | **0** | **1 slot**: Standard Overlay only | Light-compatible powers *(blocks Speed, Force Armor, Stasis, Storm, Kill, Drain Life)* |
| **Medium Armor** | `6–7` → **`5–6`** | `+2/+3` → **`+3 to +6`** | **DR `2/-`** | **2 exclusive slots**: Combined Overlay + Combined Underlay | Medium-compatible powers *(Heal, Revitalize, Barrier, Energy Resistance, Plague, Push/Wave, Battle Meditation, Enlightenment…)* |
| **Heavy Armor** | `8–9` → **`7–8`** | `0/+1` → **`0 to +2`** | **DR `4/-`** | **1 slot**: Standard Underlay only | Heavy-compatible powers *(Force Body, Throw Lightsaber, Force Resistance, Drain Force, Deflection, Prestige Ultimates)* |

> **No More Light Armor Exceptions:** **Armored Flight Suits (Ubese)** and **Zeison Sha / Jal Shey Armors** no longer bypass Force power restrictions as they did in vanilla — they now follow the exact same rules as all other Light Armors.

> **Droid Plating** (T3-M4, HK-47, G0-T0): unchanged from vanilla.

## Why Does Medium Armor Have Exclusive Upgrades?

Medium armor is designed around a distinct identity as **tactical, high-tech battle gear**: while Light armor prioritizes agility and Heavy armor focuses on raw plating, Medium armor's modular frame is built to integrate complex combined components. At the workbench, each category upgrades differently:
- **Light Armor (1 slot — Standard Overlay)**: access to the 18 standard overlays (Defense, evasion, elemental resistances, skills).
- **Heavy Armor (1 slot — Standard Underlay)**: access to the 18 standard underlays (physical DR, STR/CON, regeneration, critical/drain immunity).
- **Medium Armor (2 slots — Exclusive Combined Upgrades)**: simply wearing Medium armor lets you install **both a Dual Overlay AND a Dual Underlay** (24 exclusive pieces that combine two standard upgrades into one, no feat required). Medium armor is the ultimate tech and versatility platform!

## The 3 Armor Feat Trees

Each armor category now has its own **3-tier feat tree** (Levels 1, 8, and 15). Bonuses apply whenever you wear armor of the matching category.

### 1. Ballistic Evasion *(Light Armor — Mobility & Evasion)*
*Ideal for agile duelists and scouts who rely on avoiding incoming hits altogether.*

| Tier | Prerequisites | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Ballistic Evasion I** | Lvl 1+, Light Armor Proficiency | **`+10%` evasion**, **`+1 AC`** |
| **Ballistic Evasion II** | Lvl 8+, Tier I, Guardian / Sentinel / prestiges *(or Soldier/Scout/Scoundrel)* | **`+20%` evasion**, **`+2 AC`** |
| **Ballistic Evasion III** | Lvl 15+, Tier II, Weapon Master / Marauder / Watchman / Assassin *(or Soldier/Scout/Scoundrel Lvl 15+; Visas Lvl 15+)* | **`+30%` evasion**, **`+3 AC`** |

### 2. Tactical Armor *(Medium Armor — Shields & Stealth)*
*Ideal for engineers, hybrids, and stealth builds: supercharges energy shields and enables belt-free stealth in Medium armor.*

| Tier | Prerequisites | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Tactical Armor I** | Lvl 1+, Medium Armor Proficiency | **`DR 3/-`**, **`+1 AC`**, **shield duration `+100%`**, **stealth without a stealth belt** *(while in Medium armor)* |
| **Tactical Armor II** | Lvl 8+, Tier I, Sentinel / Watchman / Assassin *(or Scout/Scoundrel/Tech Specialist)* | **`DR 4/-`**, **`+2 AC`**, **shield absorption doubled (`×2`)**, grants **Stealth Run** *(while in Medium armor)* |
| **Tactical Armor III** | Lvl 15+, Tier II, Watchman / Assassin *(or Scout/Scoundrel/Tech Specialist Lvl 15+; Visas Lvl 15+)* | **`DR 5/-`**, **`+3 AC`**, **shield absorption tripled (`×3`)** |

### 3. Juggernaut *(Heavy Armor — Pure Tank)*
*Ideal for frontline bruisers who want to shrug off punishment.*

| Tier | Prerequisites | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Juggernaut I** | Lvl 1+, Heavy Armor Proficiency | **`DR 3/-`**, **`+10%` immunity to all damage** |
| **Juggernaut II** | Lvl 8+, Tier I, Guardian / Weapon Master / Marauder *(or Soldier)* | **`DR 4/-`**, **`+20%` immunity to all damage** |
| **Juggernaut III** | Lvl 15+, Tier II, Weapon Master / Marauder *(or Soldier Lvl 15+)* | **`DR 5/-`**, **`+30%` immunity to all damage** |

> **How much can a Juggernaut soak?**
> Combining native Heavy Armor DR (`4/-`), *Juggernaut III* (`5/-`), and *Improved Toughness* (`2/-`) gives **`DR 11/-` + `30%` immunity to all damage** (with no artificial cap on gear resistances). Against squads of regular enemies, you become virtually indestructible!
