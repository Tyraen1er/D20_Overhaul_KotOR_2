## Class & Prestige Class Access (Tiers II and III)

Now that every feat tree has been introduced, here is the full reference table showing which classes can select **Tier II (Lvl 8+)** and **Tier III (Lvl 15+)** feats:

| Feat Tree | Tier I | Tier II (Lvl 8+) | Tier III (Lvl 15+) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attribute Specializations (6 trees)** | All classes | All classes | All classes |
| **Stance: Dueling** *(Lvl 1 / 4 / 8)* | All classes | All classes *(Lvl 4+)* | All classes *(Lvl 8+)* |
| **Stances: Ambidexterity, Heavy Weapon, High-Rate** *(Lvl 1 / 4 / 8)* | All classes | All classes *(Lvl 4+)* | **All except Force classes** *(Lvl 8+; locked for Consular, Jedi Master, Sith Lord)* |
| **Weapon Specializations (8 families)** | All classes | Guardian, Sentinel & their prestiges *(+ non-Jedi)* | **Weapon Master**, **Sith Marauder** *(+ non-Jedi Lvl 15+)* |
| **Armor: Juggernaut** *(Heavy)* | All (with Heavy Armor Prof.) | Guardian, Weapon Master, Marauder *(+ Soldier)* | **Weapon Master**, **Sith Marauder** *(+ Soldier Lvl 15+)* |
| **Armor: Tactical Armor** *(Medium)* | All (with Medium Armor Prof.) | Sentinel, Watchman, Assassin *(+ Scout, Scoundrel, Tech Specialist)* | **Jedi Watchman**, **Sith Assassin** *(+ Scout, Scoundrel, Tech Specialist Lvl 15+; Visas Lvl 15+)* |
| **Armor: Ballistic Evasion** *(Light)* | All (with Light Armor Prof.) | Guardian, Sentinel & their prestiges *(+ Soldier, Scout, Scoundrel)* | **Weapon Master**, **Marauder**, **Watchman**, **Assassin** *(+ Soldier, Scout, Scoundrel Lvl 15+; Visas Lvl 15+)* |
| **Force Awakening** *(Empower, Penetrate)* | All Jedi / Sith | **Consular**, **Jedi Master**, **Sith Lord** | **Jedi Master**, **Sith Lord** *(+ Kreia Lvl 15+)* |
| **Total Evasion** | — | — | **Jedi Watchman**, **Sith Assassin** *(+ Visas Lvl 15+; Mira auto at Lvl 6)* |

## Special Rules for Companions

Your party members are fully integrated into this progression system:

### 1. Non-Jedi Companions (Soldier, Scout, Scoundrel, Tech Specialist, Droids)
Characters who remain in non-Jedi classes **can select** the **Tier III** martial and armor feats suited to their class at level-up starting at **Level 15** *(without needing a prestige class)*:
- **Soldier** *(e.g., Mandalore, Handmaiden before training)*: Weapon Specialization III, *Juggernaut III*, *Ballistic Evasion III*.
- **Scout / Scoundrel** *(e.g., Mira, Atton, Hanharr)*: Weapon Specialization III, *Ballistic Evasion III*, *Tactical Armor III*.
- **Tech Specialist** *(e.g., Bao-Dur)*: Weapon Specialization III, *Tactical Armor III*.
- **Combat Droid** *(HK-47)*: Ranged Weapon Specialization III.
- **Expert Droid** *(T3-M4, G0-T0)*: Ranged Weapon Specialization II.

> Once a companion is trained as a Jedi (Atton, Bao-Dur, Disciple, Mira, Handmaiden), they follow the standard rules of their Jedi class.

### 2. Story Companion Exceptions
- **Kreia (Level 15+)**: counts as a **Jedi Master** for selectable feats at level-up, granting full access to **Force Awakening** (including Tier III).
- **Visas Marr (Level 15+)**: counts as a **Sith Assassin** for selectable feats at level-up, unlocking *Tactical Armor III*, *Ballistic Evasion III*, and *Total Evasion*.
- **Handmaiden (Echani sparring reward)**: *Battle Precognition* (which added Wisdom to Defense) is replaced by **`+10%` melee evasion** *(avoiding overlap with the new Matukai Wisdom specialization)*.
