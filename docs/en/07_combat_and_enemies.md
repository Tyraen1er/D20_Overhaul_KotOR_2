> **Reminder (Stealth):** your **first attack** within **6 seconds** of leaving stealth gains **`+50` Attack** (guaranteed hit) and **duplicates its entire damage roll (including Sneak Attack dice)** on a **second line** in the combat log to simulate a guaranteed critical hit.

## Combat Pacing & Bosses

Global combat balance follows one golden rule: **a boss fight should feel like a real duel, not a 2-round formality.**
- **Target boss duration:** **5 to 10 rounds** (and **10 to 15 rounds** for major finale duels like Darth Sion or Darth Traya).
- **Passive Boss & Sub-Boss Damage Reduction (DR):** scales continuously with your level as **`1 + 0.25 × Level`** *(capped at **`DR 8`** — e.g., `DR 2` at Lvl 5, `DR 5` at Lvl 16, `DR 8` at Lvl 28+)*.
- **Boss Vitality** is modestly boosted to match your growing toolkit.
- **Boss damage output is moderated** so that well-built characters (in robes, light, medium, or heavy armor) can also survive 5–10 rounds without getting one-shot.
- **Full TSLRCM compatibility:** no artificial scripted boss phases were added, keeping all vanilla and restored-content encounters completely intact.

## Tactical Guide to the 6 Enemy Families

In vanilla, nearly all organic enemies fought "naked" (no real armor or resistances), making combat feel the same everywhere. **D20 Overhaul** gives **each major enemy family** a readable defensive profile that **scales continuously with your character level** (just like the game's autobalance, regardless of planet order) and a **clear weakness** to reward tactical adaptation.

> **Golden rule:** organic enemy resistances are **partial and progressive** (scaling up to `35%` or `75%` depending on the family) so mismatched tactics are heavily penalized without ever hard-locking a build. Total `100%` immunities remain reserved for droids.

| Enemy Family | Strengths & Defenses *(scaling with your Level)* | Weaknesses | How to Counter Them |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sith & Dark Jedi** | • Wear **real defensive robes**<br>• **Force Resistance:** **`10 + 1.40 × Lvl`** *(e.g., `24` at Lvl 10, `38` at Lvl 20, `52` at Lvl 30)*<br>• **Will Save Bonus:** **`1 + 0.20 × Lvl`** *(cap **`+6`**)*<br>• Gain **Total Evasion** once you reach **Level 20+**<br>• Prioritize **Drain Force** when their FP drops below 30% | • Zero Damage Reduction<br>• Average Fortitude | • Direct lightsaber or blaster damage<br>• **Fortitude**-targeting powers (*Plague*, *Stasis*)<br>• *Force Breach* and *Penetrate Power* to punch through their defenses |
| **Soldiers & Mercenaries** | • Wear **real Light & Medium armor** (`DR 2/-` on Medium)<br>• **Energy & Fire Resistance:** **`5% + 1% × Lvl`** *(cap **`35%`**)*<br>• Roughly **1 in 3 elites** activates an **energy shield** (`50–100` VP, lootable!)<br>• Still throw grenades | • Low Will saves<br>• Vulnerable to **Physical, Ion, and Sonic** damage *(energy shields do not block physical melee)* | • Physical weapons (swords, vibroblades) / melee<br>• *Shield Disruptor* grenades<br>• Mind powers (*Insanity*) and *Whirlwind* |
| **Mandalorians** | • Wear **real Heavy armor** (native `DR 4/-`)<br>• **Energy (blasters/sabers) & Fire Resistance:** **`30% + 1.5% × Lvl`** *(cap **`75%`** — e.g., `45%` at Lvl 10, `60%` at Lvl 20, `75%` at Lvl 30)*<br>• Elite shields and **stims** activated at combat start (`+2 STR/CON`) | • **Zero Force Resistance**<br>• Low Will saves<br>• Vulnerable to **Physical, Ion, and Sonic** damage | • Force powers (*Storm*, *Insanity*, *Stasis*)<br>• Physical weapons (swords, vibroblades) & **Armor Break** (*Traditional* weapons)<br>• Ion / Sonic weapons |
| **Beasts & Creatures** | • **Physical-only DR:** **`1 + 0.50 × Lvl`** *(cap **`DR 15/-`** — e.g., `6` at Lvl 10, `11` at Lvl 20, `15` at Lvl 30; zero DR against Energy)*<br>• High **Fortitude**<br>• **Fear immunity** on major predators<br>• Slow out-of-combat regeneration (apex) | • **Very low Will saves**<br>• **Zero DR or resistance against Energy, Electrical, or Fire** | • **Lightsabers, blasters**, *Lightning / Storm*<br>• Plasma / fire grenades<br>• Mind control & *Beast Confusion* |
| **Droids** | • Keep their classic **mechanical immunities** (mind, poison, drain)<br>• Roughly **1 in 3 elite war/assault droids** activates a **deflector shield** (`50–60` VP) | • **Ion**, **Electrical**, and anti-droid powers | • **Ion** weapons & grenades<br>• *Disable / Destroy Droid*, *Lightning* |
| **Sith Assassins & Stalkers** | • **Stealth** + *Uncanny Dodge*<br>• **Evasion:** **`10% + 0.5% × Lvl`** *(cap **`25%`**)*<br>• **Reflex Save Bonus:** **`1 + 0.15 × Lvl`** *(cap **`+5`**)* | • Low VP, zero DR<br>• **Low Fortitude** | • **Fortitude** powers (*Plague*, *Stasis*)<br>• Crowd control & direct damage |
