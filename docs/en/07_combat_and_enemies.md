> **Reminder (Stealth):** your **first attack** within **6 seconds** of leaving stealth gains **`+50` Attack** (guaranteed hit) and **duplicates its entire damage roll (including Sneak Attack dice)** on a **second line** in the combat log to simulate a guaranteed critical hit.

## Combat Pacing & Bosses

Global combat balance follows one golden rule: **a boss fight should feel like a real duel, not a 2-round formality.**
- **Target boss duration:** **5 to 10 rounds** (and **10 to 15 rounds** for major finale duels like Darth Sion or Darth Traya).
- **Passive Boss & Sub-Boss Damage Reduction (DR):**
  - Early game: **`DR 2`**
  - Mid game: **`DR 5`**
  - Late game: **`DR 8`**
- **Mid-game boss Vitality** is modestly boosted to match your growing toolkit.
- **Boss damage output is moderated** so that well-built characters (in robes, light, medium, or heavy armor) can also survive 5–10 rounds without getting one-shot.
- **Full TSLRCM compatibility:** no artificial scripted boss phases were added, keeping all vanilla and restored-content encounters completely intact.

## Tactical Guide to the 6 Enemy Families

In vanilla, nearly all organic enemies fought "naked" (no real armor or resistances), making combat feel the same everywhere. **D20 Overhaul** gives **each major enemy family** a readable defensive profile and a **clear weakness** to reward tactical adaptation.

> **Golden rule:** organic enemy resistances are **partial** (`25%`, `50%`, `75%`) so mismatched tactics are penalized without ever hard-locking a build. Total 100% immunities remain reserved for droids.

| Enemy Family | Strengths & Defenses | Weaknesses | How to Counter Them |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sith & Dark Jedi** | • Wear **real defensive robes**<br>• **Force Resistance** (`15 + Lvl` Knights / `20 + Lvl` Masters)<br>• Bonus **Will saves** (`+2 / +4 / +6` by rank)<br>• Gain **Total Evasion** at **Level 20+**<br>• Prioritize **Drain Force** when their FP drops below 30% | • Zero Damage Reduction<br>• Average Fortitude | • Direct lightsaber or blaster damage<br>• **Fortitude**-targeting powers (*Plague*, *Stasis*)<br>• *Force Breach* and *Penetrate Power* to punch through their defenses |
| **Soldiers & Mercenaries** | • Wear **real Light & Medium armor** (`DR 2/-` on Medium)<br>• Roughly **1 in 3 elites** (Mid/Late game) activates an **energy shield** (`50–100` VP, lootable!)<br>• Still throw grenades | • Low Will saves<br>• Energy shields do not block melee | • Melee / lightsabers<br>• *Shield Disruptor* grenades<br>• Mind powers (*Insanity*) and *Whirlwind* |
| **Mandalorians** | • Wear **real Heavy armor** (native `DR 4/-`)<br>• **Energy (blasters/sabers) & Fire resistance** (`25%` Mid / `35%` Late)<br>• Elite shields and **stims** activated at combat start (`+2 STR/CON`) | • **Zero Force Resistance**<br>• Low Will saves<br>• Vulnerable to **Physical, Ion, and Sonic** damage | • Force powers (*Storm*, *Insanity*, *Stasis*)<br>• Physical weapons (swords, vibroblades) & **Armor Break** (*Traditional* weapons)<br>• Ion / Sonic weapons |
| **Beasts & Creatures** | • **Physical & Energy DR** (`2 / 4 / 6`) on large beasts<br>• High **Fortitude**<br>• **Fear immunity** on apex predators<br>• Slow out-of-combat regeneration | • **Very low Will saves**<br>• No % resistance to Energy, Electrical, or Fire | • *Lightning / Storm*, lightsabers, blasters<br>• Plasma / fire grenades<br>• Mind control & *Beast Confusion* |
| **Droids** | • Keep their classic **mechanical immunities** (mind, poison, drain)<br>• Roughly **1 in 3 elite war/assault droids** (Mid/Late game) activates a **deflector shield** (`50–60` VP) | • **Ion**, **Electrical**, and anti-droid powers | • **Ion** weapons & grenades<br>• *Disable / Destroy Droid*, *Lightning* |
| **Sith Assassins & Stalkers** | • **Stealth** + *Uncanny Dodge*<br>• High **Reflex saves**<br>• **Ranged evasion `20–25%`** (Mid/Late game) | • Low VP, zero DR<br>• **Low Fortitude** | • Close into **melee**<br>• **Fortitude** powers (*Plague*, *Stasis*) |
