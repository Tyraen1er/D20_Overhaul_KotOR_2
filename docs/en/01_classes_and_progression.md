## New Progression Rhythm (Feats ↔ Force Powers)

In vanilla KotOR 2, a Jedi Guardian or Sith Marauder ended up with nearly 30 Force powers (almost as many as a Consular), while a Consular drowned in ~40 powers, half of which were filler.

**D20 Overhaul** establishes a **clean inverse symmetry** across the three Jedi and Sith class archetypes, calibrated up to **level 30**:

| Class Family | Power Gain Rate | Total Powers (Lvl 30) | Feat Gain Rate | Total Feats (Lvl 30) | Primary Role |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Jedi Guardian** / **Weapon Master** / **Sith Marauder** | 1 every 3 levels *(+2 at Lvl 1)* | **12** | ~2 every 3 levels + bonuses | **22** | **Martial Master** |
| **Jedi Sentinel** / **Jedi Watchman** / **Sith Assassin** | 1 every 2 levels | **16** | ~3 every 5 levels | **18** | **Tactical Hybrid** |
| **Jedi Consular** / **Jedi Master** / **Sith Lord** | 4 every 5 levels | **24** | 1 every 2 levels | **15** | **Force Master** |

> **Key takeaways:**
> - A **Guardian** picks Force powers selectively (essential buffs and key utilities) but enjoys a huge budget of **22 feats** to master weapons, stances, and armor.
> - A **Consular** gets **15 feats** (plenty of room for the new **Force Awakening** feats and an attribute specialization) alongside a deep repertoire of **24 powers**.
> - A **Sentinel** offers a balanced middle ground between combat, skills, medium armor, and Force powers.

## Minimum Character Level Gates for Feats

To keep progression smooth from early to late game, feat trees unlock progressively by **character level**:
- **Core martial feats** (*Active attacks*, *Toughness*, the **4 Combat Stances**):
  - Tier I: **Level 1+**
  - Tier II: **Level 4+**
  - Tier III: **Level 8+**
- **Major specialization trees** (**Attribute Specializations**, **Weapon Specializations**, **Armor Trees**, **Force Awakening** *Empower* & *Penetrate*):
  - Tier I: **Level 1+**
  - Tier II: **Level 8+**
  - Tier III: **Level 15+**
  *(Exception: **Extend Power** is available from Level 1.)*
